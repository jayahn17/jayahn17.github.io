Builder Portfolio

I am a UC Berkeley M.S. in Mechanical Engineering building robotics systems across controls, robot learning, embedded sensing, simulation, and deployable hardware. This portfolio is organized around shipped prototypes and tested demos rather than coursework alone: role-conditioned diffusion policies that scale from one arm to a four-arm LLM coordinator, humanoid teleoperation infrastructure, autonomous ocean-drone trials, microfluidic field hardware, LiDAR-to-3D-asset perception pipelines, MPC planners, and legged-robot locomotion.

Quick links: GitHub · CV · About · Project videos below

Builder Fit

  • Full-stack tinkering: Python for data pipelines, robot-learning workflows, and automation; comfort diving into C++/ROS robotics interfaces when the prototype demands it; MATLAB/Simulink for control validation; hands-on embedded sensing, XBee telemetry, Dynamixel servos, microcontroller hardware, and NVIDIA Isaac/MuJoCo simulation stacks.
  • Perception & 3D: LiDAR and RGB-D sensing on device (ARKit depth + pose), point-cloud and mesh processing (RANSAC plane fitting, voxel clustering, ICP registration, Poisson and TSDF reconstruction), open-vocabulary object detection and segmentation, Gaussian splatting and photogrammetry, plus the verification layer that decides when a reconstruction is trustworthy enough to act on.
  • Bias for action: I build the smallest testable prototype, instrument it, run the demo, inspect the logs, and revise quickly when sensors, drivers, geometry, or constraints fail.
  • Location: Bay Area based (Berkeley / Santa Clara), open to robotics roles and internships.

What to Review First

  • Robot learning (flagship): ICON Lab role-conditioned manipulation. Skill-decomposed diffusion policies achieved at least 95% success on one arm, then transferred unchanged to two arms and a four-arm pick-and-place system coordinated by an LLM.
  • Simulation infrastructure: Khameleon humanoid control. The project covers 13-DOF dual-arm teleoperation and Isaac Sim/Lab training workflows.
  • Autonomous systems: CAPTAIN ocean drone. The platform evolved through more than 50 field trials involving telemetry, sensing, navigation, and control.
  • 3D perception end-to-end: CrateScanner → assetpipe. The system connects LiDAR/RGB-D capture, point-cloud segmentation, mesh reconstruction, Gaussian splatting, and a dashboard that determines which of five reconstructions is safe to measure. The Swift/ARKit capture app is documented separately.
  • Render-to-sim pipeline: MSC 3D reconstruction. This workflow uses WorldLab.ai, NVIDIA 3DGRUT, and Isaac Lab to generate and validate simulation assets.
  • Algorithms to demos: MPC truck-trailer planner and spider robot locomotion. These projects demonstrate constrained planning, kinematics, and video-backed validation.
  • Thermal hardware (class): ME226 iced espresso machine. A two-stage liquid-cooling system reduced a 73 °C shot to 11.3 °C in the cup without ice or dilution.
